<objective>
Add deterministic coverage baseline comparison and suite-level benchmark evidence for representative transformer, MLP, and conv/normalization workload coverage.
</objective>

<source_audit>
GOAL: Turns the per-candidate coverage summary into workload-level evidence that can prove improved GPU-resident regions without depending on local timing thresholds.
REQ GPUCOV-01: Covered by suite-level coverage rendering.
REQ GPUCOV-02: Covered by representative workload names, baseline comparison fields, and tests asserting better-or-equal coverage behavior.
D-04 through D-06: Covered by using `StandardWorkloads` and deterministic baseline fixtures.
</source_audit>

<threat_model>
T-13-05: Coverage comparison could become a hidden speed benchmark and fail nondeterministically. Mitigation: compare selected region length, CPU materialization count, fallback count, and device handoff count; do not gate on raw timing.
T-13-06: Local benchmark/profile output could become the baseline source. Mitigation: baseline contracts live in checked-in test/report records, not under `profiles/platform/.../tuning/abc/*`.
T-13-07: The suite could skip required representative workload categories while still passing. Mitigation: tests must assert exact workload names for transformer block, MLP, and conv or normalization.
</threat_model>
