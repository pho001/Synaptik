<threat_model>
T-09-01: A shared ABI descriptor could accidentally expose backend-native handle objects or public Tensor residency. Mitigation: descriptor stores only `backendId`, `accessMode`, and an opaque `nativeHandleIdentity` string, constructed from `DeviceBufferBinding`.
T-09-02: Physical span math could overflow and silently under-report backing storage requirements. Mitigation: physical span uses checked arithmetic and returns `AcceleratorLayoutAbiV2StatusCode.PHYSICAL_SPAN_OVERFLOW` or throws in descriptor factory tests.
</threat_model>
