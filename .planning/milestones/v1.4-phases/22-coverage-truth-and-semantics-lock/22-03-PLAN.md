## Objective

Add or update representative workload baselines so later phases can prove CPU exits went down for the actual target families.

## Target Workloads

- Reduction-heavy chain: matmul or elementwise producer followed by `SUM`/`MEAN`/`REDUCE_MIN`/`REDUCE_MAX`.
- Normalization flow: `LAYER_NORM` and `RMS_NORM` with gamma/beta where applicable.
- Transformer attention: forward SDPA or decomposed attention hot path.
- Loss/indexing: index-target cross entropy or NLL loss plus gather/scatter-style dependencies.
- Conv/pool: small deterministic conv/pool flow.
- BOOL compare mask: compare output feeding `WHERE` or mask-adjacent consumer.
