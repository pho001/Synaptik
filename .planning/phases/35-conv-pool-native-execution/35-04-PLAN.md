- Requirements are checked complete only after native parity and coverage evidence exist.

## Threat Model

- **Threat:** Coverage flips to supported before native proof.
  **Mitigation:** Only update hard gates for variants with native parity tests.
- **Threat:** Local tuning output is accidentally committed.
  **Mitigation:** Stage files explicitly and leave `profiles/platform/...` dirty artifacts unstaged.
