    - `fromRuntimeConfig(RuntimeConfig runtimeConfig, ComputeBackend backend)`

    Required formula:
    - `presetName` is `PROFILE_DERIVED`.
    - `minimumEstimatedWork` is `runtimeConfig.accelerator().forBackend(backend).minimumEstimatedWork()` or `0` when runtime/backend is null.
    - `contiguousMaterializeThreshold` is `runtimeConfig.kernel().cpu().contiguousMaterializeThreshold()` or `4096` when runtime is null.
    - `scale = 4096.0d / Math.max(1024, contiguousMaterializeThreshold)`.

    - `dispatchOverhead = conservative.dispatchOverhead() + Math.min(10_000.0d, minimumEstimatedWork * 0.001d)`.
    - `uploadBytePenalty = conservative.uploadBytePenalty() * scale`.
    - `downloadBytePenalty = conservative.downloadBytePenalty() * scale`.
    - `layoutFallbackBytePenalty = conservative.layoutFallbackBytePenalty() * scale`.
