T-4-01: A graph autotune candidate could silently mutate platform thresholds and make a workload winner non-portable. Mitigation: graph candidates must declare knob assignments and pass `TuningKnobOwnership.validateGraphWorkload(...)`.
T-4-02: A calibration family could start tuning graph policy and make platform defaults workload-specific. Mitigation: calibration candidates continue to validate family-owned knobs and also pass platform/dtype ownership checks.
T-4-03: New knobs could be introduced without classification. Mitigation: tests assert all generated candidate knob assignments have a known owner.
</threat_model>
