# Historical documentation and qualification

These inventories preserve earlier implementation/qualification descriptions. Their words
“current”, “planned” and “unsupported” describe those earlier slices, not today's product.
They are maintenance evidence, not a learning path or a second API authority.

| Planned/implemented Tensor status inventory | [Historical status](reference/tensor/historical-status.md) |
| Operation-by-operation Compiler/backend slices | [Operation qualification](reference/compiler/operation-qualification.md) |
| Geometry and normalization Compiler/backend slices | [Geometry qualification](reference/compiler/geometry-qualification.md) |

and [architecture authority index](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) for present decisions. The
[completed audit](../planning/public-release-audit-2026-11-04.md) binds its stated source/artifacts;
older receipts elsewhere do not requalify later changes. [Design records](../design/README.md)
and [planning](../planning/README.md) remain separate decision and coordination histories.
